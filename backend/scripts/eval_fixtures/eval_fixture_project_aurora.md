# Project Aurora Launch Brief

## Overview

Project Aurora is GraphMind's internal codename for the Q3 2026
knowledge-graph visualization redesign. The project began on 2026-04-01
and is scheduled to ship on 2026-09-15.

## Budget

The approved budget for Project Aurora is $184,000. Of that, $52,000 is
allocated to contractor design work and the remainder covers engineering
time.

## Leadership

Project Aurora is led by Elena Rusev.

## Milestones

The first internal milestone, the interactive prototype, is due
2026-06-30. The second milestone, the closed beta, is due 2026-08-01.
